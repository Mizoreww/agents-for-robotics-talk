Gemini Robotics 2
Our most advanced VLA model: capable of intelligently controlling any type of robot, from tabletop robots to full humanoids

[#intro]
Gemini Robotics 2 can operate robotic bodies of all sizes and shapes to perform complex tasks. Including dexterous manipulation of hands and grippers to full control of a humanoid body – from feet to fingertips.
[#capabilities]
Capabilities
Gemini Robotics 2 can power any type of robot to think and then interact with the physical world around them.
General
Controls robots of any shape or size, scaling its intelligence from arms to complex humanoid bodies.

Intelligent whole-body control
Controls entire humanoid bodies from feet to fingertips. Dynamically balances a robot’s centre of gravity – so, instead of simply reaching from a static position, a robot can safely step, squat, and bend to navigate cluttered, real-world spaces.
Advanced dexterity
Unlocks human-level dexterity to use complex, 22-degree-of-freedom hands and grippers.
